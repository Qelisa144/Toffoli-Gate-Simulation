# 🌌 About Me   

This is Elisa, a Mexican woman who has always felt a deep call to transcend. Since I was a child, I have felt that I was meant for something bigger. I have always loved mathematics, creating, and finding new ways to do things.  

When studying Chemical Engineering, I found great joy in leading projects and being chosen by excellent professors to represent my whole group. I have always been able to recognize people’s strengths and help a team reach its full potential while keeping the project focused on results. My favorite subjects were Analytical Chemistry and Electrochemistry because I loved the theoretical challenge.

After finishing my degree, I realized that research in Mexico was often undervalued, so I followed a social path in the Human Resources department at Ford Motor Company. There, I applied my problem-solving mindset to improve processes while working closely with people, merging the technical with the human side. I was seen as a team player, always proposing improvements while letting others shine.  

One day in Australia, I met a mystic woman who told me: **“You will work in Quantum Computing Field”** At that moment, it sounded impossible, but her words planted a seed. Two years later, that spark was still alive. I decided to take my first course in quantum computing with QUCAN, and everything made sense. I realized how deeply connected this field is to my background in Chemical Engineering.  

**Eureka!** I had found my place.  

Since February, I have dedicated myself to exploring the wonders of quantum computing. This field ignites my days and gives me purpose. I truly believe that its breakthroughs will impact not only a few people but millions.  

Now, as I **move to Europe — actually to Romania — to be closer to the quantum community and build great connections**, I feel ready to take the next step. I want to keep growing alongside others who share the same passion for this transformative science.  

I sincerely hope to be part of this mentorship program. I am confident that with your guidance, I can reach my full potential and contribute meaningfully to the future of this field.  

With much respect,
